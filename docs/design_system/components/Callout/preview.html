How to read these charts

Error vs measurements — anytime curves; the sweep is the practical baseline.
Convergence — survival curves, adaptive locators only.
Each ellipse is the 1σ covariance of two parameters' posterior. A tilted ellipse means the two are correlated.
Viewing at step 61 — primary converged.
7 resample + jitter steps. An uncertainty jump at one of these is expected, not an anomaly.
